# Product Guidelines

## Design Philosophy
The design of JPE Mod Translator 2.0 should evoke a **Sophisticated & Cinematic** feel, heavily inspired by the Apple TV user experience. The interface must be elegant, high-end, and immersive, elevating the technical task of mod translation into a premium experience.

## Visual Hierarchy & Layout
- **Focus and Depth:** The primary layout principle is to use the z-axis and subtle scaling to indicate selection and focus.
- **Cinematic Staging:** UI elements should "float" and breathe. Content should take center stage, with chrome and navigation receding when not in use.
- **Layering:** Use semi-transparent materials and blurs to maintain context while focusing on the active task.

## Content Strategy
- **Minimalist Labeling:** Use clear, concise labels and meaningful iconography to reduce cognitive load. Avoid clutter.
- **Contextual Instruction:** Provide brief, helpful explanations only when necessary (e.g., empty states, complex diagnostics) to guide the user without overwhelming them.

## Visual Identity
- **Color Palette:** **Deep Dark Mode**. Utilize true blacks and deep grays as the canvas. Use vibrant, high-contrast accents sparingly to denote focus states, errors, or primary actions.
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif typography that supports high readability at various sizes. (Think San Francisco or Inter).

## Interaction & Motion
- **Fluid & Responsive:** Animations should be smooth and physics-based, bridging the gap between states naturally.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Incorporate detailed feedback for interactions. Hover states, button presses, and toggles should feel tactile and responsive, adding to the sense of quality.
