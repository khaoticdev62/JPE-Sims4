# ⚡ JPE Studio Quick Start (5 Minutes)

**The fastest way to translate your first Sims 4 mod!**

---

## 30 Seconds: What Is This?

JPE Studio takes your Sims 4 mod and helps you translate it to other languages in minutes, not hours.

```
Your Mod (English)
        ↓
📍 JPE finds all the text
        ↓
✏️ You translate it
        ↓
📦 Your translated mod is ready!
```

---

## 1 Minute: Install It

**Option A: Windows (Easiest)**

```powershell
# 1. Open Command Prompt (search for "cmd")
# 2. Paste this and press Enter:

pip install jpe-sims4-studio

# 3. Wait for "Successfully installed"
# 4. Done! ✓
```

**Option B: Mac**

```bash
# Open Terminal and paste:
pip3 install jpe-sims4-studio
```

**Option C: Already Have It?**

Just open it:
- Windows: Press Windows key, type `jpe-studio`, press Enter
- Mac: Open Terminal, type `jpe-studio`, press Enter

---

## 2 Minutes: Load Your Mod

1. **Click "New Project"** (big purple button)

2. **Select your mod folder or ZIP file**
   - Find where your Sims 4 mods are stored
   - Click on a mod
   - Click "Open"

3. **Wait for "Scan complete!"**
   - Takes 10-30 seconds usually
   - JPE is analyzing your mod

4. **You'll see:** "87 segments found" (or whatever number)
   - This means "87 pieces of text need translating"

✅ **Your mod is loaded!**

---

## 3 Minutes: Translate

1. **Click the "Translate" tab** (left sidebar)

2. **Click first segment** in the list

3. **Type your translation** in the right box
   ```
   Source (English): "Welcome to my mod!"
   Your translation: "¡Bienvenido a mi mod!" (Spanish example)
   ```

4. **Press Tab** to move to next

5. **Repeat for a few more** (or all if you want!)

✅ **You're translating!**

---

## 4 Minutes: Check Your Work

1. **Click "Issues" tab** (left sidebar)

2. **You see any problems:**
   - 🔴 Red = Must fix
   - 🟡 Yellow = Nice to fix
   - 🔵 Blue = Just info

3. **Fix red ones** by going back to Translate and editing

✅ **Your work is validated!**

---

## 5 Minutes: Build Your Translated Mod

1. **Click "Build" tab** (left sidebar)

2. **Click "Build Now"** (purple button)

3. **Wait for "Build complete!"**

4. **Click "Open Folder"**
   - Your translated mod is there!
   - Ready to use in Sims 4

✅ **Congratulations! Your first translation is done!** 🎉

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to translate everything?**
A: Nope! Translate as much as you want. JPE tracks what's done.

**Q: Can I use AI to help translate?**
A: Yes! Click the ✨ icon next to translation box for AI suggestions.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: Easy! Just fix it and rebuild. Your original mod is never touched.

**Q: Can I work with a team?**
A: Yes! Export your project (Projects → Right-click → Export), send to team members, they translate, you merge it back.

**Q: Where's my translated mod saved?**
A: It's in JPE_Builds folder on your computer. JPE shows you the location when done.
